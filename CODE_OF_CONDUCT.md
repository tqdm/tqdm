# Conduct

International law supersedes all codes of conduct, including this one.
Authors of codes of conduct presuming the authority to create new laws should be brought to justice.

If you still insist on wringing *advice* from a random source on the internet:
Focus on the project. Be professional.
[Report inappropriate conduct](https://help.github.com/en/articles/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Contributions may be reworded, censored, and/or reported as abuse without notice.

## No CLA

A contributor licence agreement (CLA) would allow enforcement of additional rules, but would also be a scary barrier for new contributors. We don't want to be scary. We love you!
